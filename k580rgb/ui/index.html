Side strips

These are the keyboard's built-in effects, so they keep running with the app closed. Every change is saved on the keyboard. The app waits until you stop adjusting and only sends what changed. FN + PAUSE on the keyboard also cycles the side-strip modes.

Click or drag across keys to paint. Right-click a key to pick up its color. Applying switches the keyboard to its Custom effect and stores the layout in the active hardware profile. To fill the layout with a gradient or rainbow, open Live effects and use “Save frame to keyboard”.

Macros and key assignments are saved on the keyboard and work without the app. Fn combinations (Fn + F1–F12 media keys, Fn + arrows, …) always keep working.

Key assignments

KeyDoes

Click any key on the keyboard above to remap it too. By default G1–G5 play what you recorded with REC on the keyboard; REC recording keeps working for G-keys left on Default. The Fn key can't be remapped, so the Fn shortcuts always stay available.

Macros

#ActionKeyThen wait (ms)

Macros play keys and key combinations with delays (steps of about 10 ms, up to 39 s per step) and can repeat. The browser can't record the Windows key, Print Screen or Fn; add those with Add. Text is typed with US-layout keys. Keys still held at the end of a macro are released automatically.
